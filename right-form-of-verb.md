# Class 5: Right Form of Verbs (Basic Rules)

## Rule 1: Subject-Verb Agreement
Subject যদি Third Person Singular Number (He, She, It, যেকোনো একক নাম) হয় এবং বাক্যটি Present Indefinite Tense হয়, তবে verb-এর শেষে **s** বা **es** যোগ হয়।

* Example: He (play) football.  
  👉 **Answer:** He **plays** football.
* Example: The boy (read) a book.  
  👉 **Answer:** The boy **reads** a book.

---

## Rule 2: Universal Truth & Habitual Fact
চিরন্তন সত্য (Universal truth) বা অভ্যাসগত কাজ (Habitual fact) বোঝালে Present Indefinite Tense হয়।

* Example: The sun (rise) in the east.  
  👉 **Answer:** The sun **rises** in the east.
* Example: Ice (float) on water.  
  👉 **Answer:** Ice **floats** on water.
