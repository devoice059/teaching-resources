# Class 5: Making WH-Questions (Primary Rules)

To make a question from an underlined word, follow this basic structure:
**WH-Word + Auxiliary Verb + Subject + Main Verb + Remaining words + ?**

---

## Key WH-Words & Uses
* **Who (কে / কারা):** Used for persons (subject).
* **What (কী / পেশা):** Used for things, actions, or professions.
* **When (কখন):** Used for time.
* **Where (কোথায়):** Used for places.
* **Why (কেন):** Used for reasons.
* **How (কেমন / কীভাবে / বয়স / দূরত্ব):** Used for manner, condition, age, or quantity.

---

## Step-by-Step Examples

1. **Statement:** **Sima** is at home today.  
   👉 **Question:** **Who** is at home today?

2. **Statement:** Babul gets up **at 6:00 AM**.  
   👉 **Question:** **When** does Babul get up?

3. **Statement:** They live **in Chattogram**.  
   👉 **Question:** **Where** do they live?

4. **Statement:** She goes to school **by bus**.  
   👉 **Question:** **How** does she go to school?

5. **Statement:** Tamim is absent **because he is ill**.  
   👉 **Question:** **Why** is Tamim absent?

---

## Practice Exercise for Students
Make WH-questions from the following sentences (focusing on the bold words):
1. **Raju** is a firefighter.
2. The train leaves **at 8:30 PM**.
3. Maria cannot see **because she is visually impaired**.
4. They went to **Dhaka** yesterday.
5. He is **ten years old**.

### Answer Key
1. Who is a firefighter?
2. When does the train leave?
3. Why cannot Maria see?
4. Where did they go yesterday?
5. How old is he?
